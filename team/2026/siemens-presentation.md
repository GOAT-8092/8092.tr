### Slide 10: Neden Biz? (Why Us?)

**Çözümlerimiz (Our Solutions):**

- Uzaktan iş birliği kültürü (Remote collaboration culture)

- **Open source** approach to documentation

### 3. Community Engagement

- **B/S/H Maker's Lab**: STEM workshops for sponsor's employees' children
- **G.O.A.T. Jr.**: Mentoring FLL team at local middle school
- Not just competing, **spreading the movement**

- **Gender equality goal**: ≥ 40% female participation
- **Flexible scheduling** for academic success

- **Study hall protocols** during exam weeks
